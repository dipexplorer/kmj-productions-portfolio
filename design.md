# KMJ Productions — Design Brief

## Client Snapshot (from Instagram)

- **Brand:** KMJ Productions — "Couple Storyteller" | Photo + Films
- **Scale:** 15.3K followers, 1,349 posts, verified — genuinely established, not a beginner studio
- **Sister studio:** KMJ Fotobox (mentioned in bio) — worth a subtle cross-link, not a main feature
- **Coverage:** Pan-India availability, based in Guwahati
- **Current online presence:** Instagram + Linktree only — no dedicated site (this is the actual
  gap being solved)

## Brand Voice (pulled directly from their actual content, not invented)

Their existing work has a distinct identity already — this site should amplify it, not overwrite
it with a generic "luxury wedding studio" aesthetic:

- **Candid over posed:** piggyback shots, running-through-a-field moments, playful basketball
  photo, "one cozy night" bonfire shoot — genuine motion and joy, not stiff studio poses
- **Warm, golden, film-like color grading** in most shots — natural light, golden hour, earthy
  tones — occasional black & white for emotional/intimate moments
- **Strong use of on-image typography/captions** in their actual posts (couple names, "Days to
  go," teaser text) — this typographic storytelling habit is a real brand signature worth
  carrying into the site
- **Story-driven naming** — posts are framed as stories ("Vipul & Neha — Prewedding Teaser",
  "Shaurya & Ayushi — Candid Love Story") — the site should preserve this narrative framing
  rather than flattening everything into generic "Portfolio Image #4"

## Why NOT to reuse a generic dark-cinematic template

The earlier prompt drafted for a generic photographer (A24-style dark/moody) doesn't match KMJ's
actual visual identity, which is warmer, brighter, and more candid/joyful than cinematic-dark.
Building the site around their real photos means matching their real tone: warm, golden,
motion-filled, story-driven — not imposing a moodier aesthetic that fights their existing work.

## Visual Identity Direction

- **Palette:** warm cream/off-white base, deep warm brown or terracotta as the primary accent
  (pulled from the actual tones in their photos — warm sweaters, golden fields, sunset light),
  a small amount of near-black for grounding text/footer — avoid cold blacks or icy blues entirely
- **Typography:** a confident modern serif or elegant script accent for couple names/story
  titles (mirroring their own on-image caption style), clean sans-serif for body/UI text
- **Photography treatment:** let the candid motion speak — avoid over-cropping into rigid
  uniform grids; asymmetric, editorial layouts that mirror how they already compose Instagram
  carousels

## Site Structure

1. **Hero** — a strong candid moment (the piggyback shot or similar joy-filled image) full-bleed,
   "KMJ Productions" + "Couple Storyteller" tagline, dual CTA: "View Our Stories" / "Enquire Now"
2. **Featured Wedding Films** — horizontally scrollable film strip (Netflix-card style), since
   Photo + Films is explicitly their positioning, films need real visual weight, not an
   afterthought
3. **Portfolio Gallery** — asymmetric editorial grid, filterable (Pre-Wedding / Wedding /
   Candid Stories / Events), preserving the story-title convention ("Couple Name — Story Type")
4. **About/The Studio** — behind-the-scenes energy (they have real BTS content per their
   Instagram highlights), studio's actual story, pan-India availability mentioned clearly
5. **Packages** — tiered offering cards, Photo-only / Photo+Film / Full Story packages
6. **Testimonials** — if available from Instagram comments/DMs, else leave as a structured
   placeholder section ready for real client quotes
7. **Enquiry** — event date, event type (wedding/pre-wedding/event), package interest, contact —
   routes to WhatsApp
8. **Footer** — Instagram (@kmjproductions), sister studio (@kmjfotobox) subtle mention,
   location (Guwahati, Pan-India available), contact

## What NOT to do

- Don't default to a cold/dark cinematic mood — their real work is warm and candid, match that
- Don't flatten their story-driven post captions into generic stock portfolio labels
- Don't undersell the Films side — "Photo + Films" is their stated identity, both need real
  presence
